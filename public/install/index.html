Install from Chrome

Follow these quick steps on Android

1

Tap the three dots menu in the top-right corner

Look for the ⋮ icon at the top of your screen
2

Choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen"

Selecting "Install app" installs the full ClassMate application
3

Tap "Install" to confirm

ClassMate will appear right on your home screen

Open in Browser to Install

In-app browsers don't support direct app installation

1

Tap the three dots (⋮ or ⋯) at the top

2

Choose "Open in Chrome" or "Open in Browser"

3

Tap "Install ClassMate" in Chrome

Install ClassMate on iPhone

Follow these quick steps in Safari

1

Tap the Share button

Located at the bottom bar in Safari
2

Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"

3

Tap "Add" in the top-right corner

ClassMate will launch directly into the app from your home screen

Install on Computer

Launch ClassMate like a native desktop app

1

Look for the Install icon in the address bar

Usually an icon on the right side of the URL bar
2

Or click browser menu (⋮) > "Install ClassMate"

3

Click "Install" to confirm
